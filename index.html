A few practical questions.

Who is Taskline designed for?

Independent professionals and small teams who want a clear view of priorities, ownership and the next handoff.

Can the same approach work for solo projects?

Yes. A small board is useful even when you own every task: it keeps the next action visible when you return to the project.

How should work move between the three stages?

Use Next for work you intend to begin, In progress for active work, and Ready for a task that is complete or ready for its next handoff.

Is this a working SaaS application?

This is a landing-page demonstration for a fictional product. The board contains sample content; this template does not create accounts, store tasks or provide an online workspace.

Can I adapt the template for my own product?

Yes. The supplied HTML, styles, content and artwork are editable. Follow the included open-source license notices and replace the demo content with information about your product.
